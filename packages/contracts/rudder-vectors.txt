# Golden vectors of the rudder guard (RS1): the page (`lib/rc/safety.ts`, `lib/app/armed.ts`) and the backend (`apps/backend/rudder.pi`) must give
# the same yaw for the same inputs. One vector a line, fields separated by `|`:
#   name | option | armed | status | ageMs | throttle | yaw | expected yaw
# option: on or off (the person's "let the sticks arm and disarm"). armed: true, false or unknown (the vehicle's armed state: unknown when
# no heartbeat for 3 s). status: the last heartbeat's system_status (3 STANDBY, 4 ACTIVE, 5 CRITICAL, 1 BOOT), ageMs: how old it is.
# throttle and yaw are pulses (`own` is the radio's own throttle). The ranges are fixed: throttle 1100..1900 with a dead zone of 0 ("low" is
# up to 1180), yaw 1100 / 1500 / 1900 (the hold is at 0.8 of 400: 1180 and 1820).
# --- the option off: the hold always applies
option off, disarmed, throttle low, yaw right | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1900 | 1820
option off, disarmed, throttle low, yaw left | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
option off, armed and landed, throttle low, yaw left | off | true | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
# --- the option on: lifted when disarmed (the arm gesture), or armed and landed (the disarm gesture on the ground)
option on, disarmed, yaw right | on | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1900 | 1900
option on, disarmed, yaw left | on | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1100
option on, disarmed, any status | on | false | 4 | 100 | 1100 | 1900 | 1900
option on, armed and landed, yaw left | on | true | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1100
option on, armed and landed, heartbeat 3 s old | on | true | 3 | 3000 | 1100 | 1100 | 1100
# --- the option on, but the vehicle is not known to be on the ground: the hold stays
armed in the air (ACTIVE) | on | true | 4 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed with a failsafe (CRITICAL) | on | true | 5 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed, still booting (BOOT) | on | true | 1 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed, a status that is not known | on | true | 99 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed and STANDBY but the heartbeat is stale | on | true | 3 | 3001 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed, the heartbeat says nothing (no age) | on | true | 3 | -1 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed state not known | on | unknown | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
armed state not known, yaw right | on | unknown | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1900 | 1820
# --- the throttle is what makes the hold: it applies only while the throttle is low
throttle up, hold would apply, yaw left | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1500 | 1100 | 1100
throttle just above low | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1181 | 1100 | 1100
throttle just low | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1180 | 1100 | 1180
throttle at the minimum | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1100 | 1180
the radio's own throttle is low | off | false | 3 | 100 | own | 1900 | 1820
# --- a yaw within 0.8 of its travel is left alone, with or without the hold
yaw within the limit, right | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1820 | 1820
yaw within the limit, left | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1180 | 1180
yaw at the middle | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1500 | 1500
yaw a little over the limit | off | false | 3 | 100 | 1100 | 1821 | 1820
