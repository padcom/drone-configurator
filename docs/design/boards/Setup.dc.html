The first compass drives heading. An external compass, away from the motors and wiring, is usually the better choice.

The motors run at up to 75% throttle in this test. Take the propellers off first.

LAST RESULT, COMPASS 1
18%
Interference from the motors. Under 30% is good.

HOW TO DO IT

  1. Hold the vehicle away from metal.
  2. Rotate it so each side faces the ground.
  3. Keep going until every dot on the sphere is lit.
